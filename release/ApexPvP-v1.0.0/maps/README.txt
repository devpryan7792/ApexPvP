=== ApexPvP Maps & Schematics Directory ===
Upload your custom arena schematics here (.schem or .schematic format)!

How to use:
1. Drop your .schem or .schematic file into this 'maps/' folder (e.g. 'castle.schem').
2. In-game as OP/admin, run:
   /arena import castle.schem castle "Castle Arena"
   (The plugin automatically calculates dimensions and registers instance #0!)
3. Fine-tune your spawns:
   - Stand at player 1 spawn: /arena setspawn castle a
   - Stand at player 2 spawn: /arena setspawn castle b
   - Fly to spectator spawn: /arena setspawn castle spec
4. Your arena will immediately appear in the /duel arena selector and arena rotation!
